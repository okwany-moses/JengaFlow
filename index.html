Investment & Packages

Transparent Pricing. Powerful Digital Solutions.

From a simple business website to a fully customized management system, we offer practical digital solutions designed around your budget and business goals.

Important Pricing Conditions & Scope Transparency:

Prices are starting prices in Kenyan Shillings (KES). Final pricing depends on the agreed scope, functionality, content volume, integrations, and delivery requirements. Domain registration assistance, hosting setup, paid email subscriptions (e.g. Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailboxes), premium third-party plugins, paid APIs, payment gateway transaction charges, and ongoing maintenance are included only when explicitly stated in your formal written quotation.

WhatsApp integration includes direct click-to-chat links by default; automated chatbot messaging or the WhatsApp Business Platform/API requires separate third-party setup and meta platform messaging fees. Converted amounts in USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD are indicative estimates based on standard exchange rates.

Common Questions About Our Services

Everything you need to know about our web packages, delivery process, emails, and technical support.

First, we discuss your business goals, required sections, and content. We then provide a written quotation specifying exact deliverables, timeline, exclusions, payment milestones, and support terms. Work begins upon agreement and confirmation of the initial deposit.

A Basic one-page website typically takes 3 to 7 business days. A Standard 5-page website usually requires 7 to 14 business days, and Professional or Custom projects depend on the agreed scope, feature complexity, and prompt provision of content.

Our packages include setup and DNS configuration assistance. Domain registration fees (e.g. .com, .co.ke, .tech) and ongoing hosting fees are third-party recurring costs paid to registrars and hosting providers, unless expressly bundled into an ongoing management contract.

We assist with configuring DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM) and creating mailboxes up to your package limit (e.g., info@yourdomain.com). If you choose premium email providers such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, their per-user monthly mailbox subscriptions are billed directly by those providers.

We will link your new website to your existing domain while carefully safeguarding your existing MX records so your current business email addresses continue receiving mail without interruption.

Each package includes structured revision rounds (1 round for Basic, 2 for Standard, 3 for Professional). During each round, you can compile feedback on layout, copy, and visuals to ensure the final product represents your business.

All packages include WhatsApp click-to-chat integration for free. This allows visitors to click a button and immediately open a prefilled chat with you on their phone or computer. Automated chatbot replies and server-triggered notifications require the official WhatsApp Business Platform/API, which entails separate Meta setup and conversation fees.
